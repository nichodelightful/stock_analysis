### 2.2 FOMC 10 月 27 至 28 日會議紀要(Fed 官網 A;台北 11/19 03:00 公布;資料代理與新聞代理兩份取自同一官方頁,原文逐字引述)
- **表決:10 比 2 維持 3.75% 至 4.00%;投票維持者(紀要列示):Warsh、Williams、Barr、Bowman、Jefferson、Kashkari、Logan、Paulson、Powell、Waller;反對:Cook 與 Hammack(皆「preferred to raise the target range… by 1/4 percentage point」)。** 紀要不載個別官員理由,Cook 與 Hammack 的論點:**查不到**(Fed 與 Cleveland Fed 頁面無可用聲明文字)。
- **對 12 月的暗示(原文):** 「almost all participants judged that… further action to raise the target range… would likely be appropriate」(與 9 月 SEP 大致相符);「A couple of participants noted that an increase of more than 25 basis points could warrant consideration at the next meeting if incoming inflation data were unfavorable」;「A few participants observed that inflation could prove more persistent… a shift to a more restrictive policy stance could become necessary」。
- **「some」主張升息組(原文論點):** 「inflation had remained too high and… few signs of it abating」;「the current policy stance was not sufficiently restrictive」;**a few 警告「further delay risked allowing high inflation to become entrenched or necessitating a larger and more disruptive tightening later」;****「some」的規模大於兩張反對票(Benzinga:「In Fed language, 'some' is a bigger group than 'a few' or 'several.'」,B)。**
- **支持維持的理由(「most」支持維持):** 「Many… pointed to benefits in accumulating additional information」(包括評估 AI 投資需求持久度、融資條件、9 月升息的傳導與地緣發展);「A few… viewed the decision as a close call」;「A few… could have supported no change… provided that it was clearly understood by market participants that further tightening was likely」;「Many… stressed… the decision to maintain the target range did not reflect a weakening of the Committee's commitment」;「A couple… a gradual approach to policy firming would give businesses and households time to adjust」。
- **通膨:** 「most remarked that inflation pressures in the economy were broad based」;「The vast majority noted that high energy prices continued to contribute」;「Many… the AI buildout was a material source of inflation risk」;「Several judged that price pressures were increasingly extending beyond industries directly affected by data center construction」;「A majority noted the possibility that higher costs could be passed through more broadly and persistently」;「A few… price pressures could diminish as productive capacity and productivity increased」;「A number… greater scrutiny of the sector's returns and financing could lead to a slowing in AI-related spending」;**幕僚估計:9 月 PCE 3.6%、核心 3.1%。**
- **就業:** 「stable and close to maximum employment」;「Many pointed to signs of modest tightening」;失業率 4.2%(9 月)、近三個月平均非農新增約 5 萬;「Many observed that shortages of skilled workers needed for data center construction… had contributed to sizable wage premiums」。
